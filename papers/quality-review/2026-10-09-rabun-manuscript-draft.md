# Rabûn — Accountable Action and Governance: Scientific Manuscript Draft
**Date:** 2026-10-09. **Status:** Conceptual research proposal; no experimental evidence, completed external review, or journal submission.

## Abstract
Rabûn is proposed as the governance and responsibility component of the Zanistarast framework. It distinguishes operational performance from normative acceptability. The proposal specifies explicit policy requirements, observable outcomes, competing baselines, counterexamples and evaluation criteria. No physical law is claimed to determine moral legitimacy.

## 1. Definition and scope
The existing ontology notes define Rabûn as protection, governance and non-harm. The inspected papers folder has no independent Rabûn manuscript. This draft therefore supplies a candidate scientific structure rather than asserting an established theory. The canonical order is Ehad → Tek → Yek → Hebûn → Zanabûn → Mabûn → Rabûn → Rasterast. Rabûn is not identical to L5 task performance or L6 stability. Hebûn defines the affected system and its boundary; Zanabûn concerns the quality of evidence; Mabûn concerns constraints and resources; Rabûn concerns decision accountability; Rasterast can check implementation against declared rules.

## 2. Separate normative and empirical claims
Normative principles include fairness, accountability, procedural transparency, protection, and meaningful reconsideration of contested decisions. These are openly stated commitments, not findings derived from data. Empirical measures depend on the application and may include incident rate, severity, appeal processing time, resource use, and service reliability. An accurate predictive model cannot by itself establish that its governing policy is appropriate.

## 3. Formal proposal
For state x and permitted action a, maximize declared expected performance U(a,x) subject to separately specified and justified constraints C_k(a,x)=true and risk limits R_j(a,x)≤r_j. The thresholds r_j must be chosen through a documented governance process, not presented as universal constants. Include an explicit deferral action where evidence or authority is inadequate. Report the uncertainty of risk estimates; do not represent a probabilistic bound as a guarantee.

## 4. Falsifiable hypotheses and alternative models
H1: compared with a performance-only baseline, the proposed procedure reduces the prespecified incident severity rate in an independently held-out simulation.
H2: compared with a weighted-risk baseline, the proposed procedure improves traceability and decision reconsideration at a prespecified minimum task-completion rate.
Both hypotheses can fail. Include a stable-but-unfair system, an efficient-but-unaccountable system, and a protective-but-overrestrictive policy as negative controls. Report overall and subgroup outcomes rather than hiding variation inside a single composite score.

## 5. Proposed study
Select one bounded application domain. Register the decision unit, stakeholders, applicable rules, baseline, endpoints, severity rubric, stopping conditions and exclusion criteria before evaluation. Start with synthetic scenarios. Use identical scenarios for each policy, keep scenario families separate between development and test sets, and compare against simple alternatives. Measure uncertainty intervals, missing data and distribution shift. Any future human-participant research requires appropriate ethics and privacy review. Specify an independently auditable record of policy version, authority, decision rationale, appeals and corrections. Report unfavorable and null results.

## 6. Insert-ready paragraph for the unified manuscript
**Rabûn (governance layer).** Rabûn denotes a proposed governance process in which system actions are evaluated against explicitly justified policy constraints, observable outcomes and accountability requirements. Stability, throughput and predictive accuracy do not establish normative legitimacy. Its incremental effectiveness is an empirical hypothesis to be tested against performance-only and risk-aware baselines under prespecified conditions. Moral and theological interpretations are stated separately from experimentally measurable effects.
