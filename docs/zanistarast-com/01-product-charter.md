# Zanistarast.com — Product Charter

Status: LOCKED — Roadmap item 1

## Purpose

Zanistarast.com is the public-facing media and learning platform of the Zanistarast ecosystem. It translates eligible knowledge into accessible, human-centered experiences for a broad audience through story, image, sound, film, animation, readable prose and future interactive media.

The product is designed for ordinary visitors rather than an academic audience. Public presentation should prefer clear language, concrete situations, culturally grounded storytelling and sensory/visual explanation over unnecessary technical terminology.

## Boundary with zanistarast.org

- **zanistarast.org** remains the scientific/academic source, verification and publication layer.
- **zanistarast.com** is a separate public media product. It must not become a copy of the .org site.
- Scientific material from .org is not automatically eligible for .com. Working drafts and repository material remain source/reference material.
- Scientific material becomes eligible for public adaptation only after the required scientific, Rasterast and publication-readiness gates defined by Zanistarast are satisfied. Where a verified publication or persistent identifier exists, the public adaptation must preserve and expose that provenance.
- A .com adaptation may simplify language and change medium, but must not silently strengthen, weaken or alter the source claim, evidence status or meaning.

## Public experience principle

The default journey is:

**See → Feel → Understand → Go deeper**

A visitor may first encounter a film, animation, story, image, audio piece or other suitable medium; then receive a concise public-language explanation; then open a deeper readable treatment; and, where applicable, follow the verified source/provenance back to zanistarast.org or another legitimate source.

## Product identity

Zanistarast.com is intended to grow into an ethics-and-fitrah-centered media and learning library. Its long-term content surface may include films, short films, documentaries, animation, cartoons, AI-assisted original productions, stories, tales, audio narration, illustrations, infographics, timelines, maps and interactive works.

The architecture must therefore treat media as multiple representations of a work, not define the product as merely a video site.

## Governing constraints

This charter does not itself authorize publication. Content admission, cultural/moral visual policy, copyright and legal review, Rasterast review, Müdebbir authority, recommendation safety and the Mira production pipeline are defined by later roadmap items and remain mandatory before the relevant features are activated.

## Item 1 acceptance criteria

1. .org and .com have distinct responsibilities.
2. .com has a public, non-academic communication purpose.
3. Draft scientific material cannot flow automatically from .org to .com.
4. Public adaptation preserves source meaning and epistemic status.
5. The product is media-format agnostic and can grow beyond video.
6. The public journey is explicitly See → Feel → Understand → Go deeper.
7. Later Rasterast, legal/copyright, cultural and Müdebbir gates are not bypassed by this charter.
