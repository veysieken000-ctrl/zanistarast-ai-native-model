# Zanistarast.com — See, Feel, Understand, Deepen UX

Status: LOCKED — Roadmap item 6

## Principle

The public experience follows four progressive layers:

**See → Feel → Understand → Deepen**

This is an experience architecture, not a rigid requirement that every work contain four separate screens. A visitor should be able to enter through an accessible sensory/narrative experience and choose how far to continue without being forced through academic exposition.

## 1. See

The first encounter should make the subject immediately graspable. Depending on the work this may be film, animation, documentary, illustration, photography, audio-led visual narrative, infographic, map, timeline or another suitable medium.

Media is selected because it explains the subject well, not because every page must contain video.

The opening must avoid deceptive sensationalism, manipulative thumbnails and claims stronger than the underlying source.

## 2. Feel

Ethical understanding may involve empathy, consequence, tension, beauty, loss, responsibility, conscience, relationship and choice. Storytelling may therefore engage emotion and the senses.

Emotion must illuminate the subject rather than replace truth. The system must not manufacture false events, fake testimony or misleading scientific certainty merely to intensify a reaction.

Where fiction, dramatization, reconstruction or AI-generated scenes are used, their status must remain distinguishable in provenance/metadata and disclosed where needed for a reasonable visitor not to be misled.

## 3. Understand

Immediately below or alongside the primary work, the visitor should be able to access a concise public-language explanation:

- What is happening?
- What human/ethical question is being explored?
- What choice or consequence matters?
- What cultural/historical context is necessary?
- What should not be inferred from the work?

This layer should favor clear everyday language. Specialist terms are introduced only when they genuinely improve understanding.

## 4. Deepen

Visitors who want more may open a deeper reading layer containing, as applicable:

- expanded public-language article;
- source/provenance card;
- original work/author/culture information;
- adaptation status;
- scientific source and .org link when eligible;
- relevant Zanistarast conceptual explanation;
- uncertainty/caveats/counterevidence;
- related works and further learning.

Deepen must not be required simply to discover the real source of a potentially misleading claim; essential provenance and disclosure remain accessible.

## Progressive disclosure

On the watch/work page, the primary media remains visually central. A compact summary appears beneath it. Longer reading can expand/collapse without navigating away, while source/provenance remains reachable.

Desktop may place related works beside the primary player. Mobile should place them naturally below the primary work after essential controls/context.

## Accessibility and sensory alternatives

“Feel” must not mean dependence on one sense. The architecture should support:

- captions/subtitles for audiovisual media;
- transcripts for spoken material where practical;
- readable text alternatives;
- meaningful alt text for explanatory images;
- keyboard-operable controls;
- no essential meaning conveyed only by sound, color or animation.

Reduced-motion and other accessibility preferences should be respected by later UI implementation.

## Age/context adaptation

The same underlying ethical subject may have different public treatments for children, young people, families or adults. Adaptation may change vocabulary, pacing and examples, but must not change the source truth/status to make it easier.

## Success criteria

The product should optimize for meaningful comprehension and voluntary deeper exploration, not merely watch time. Engagement metrics may inform UX but cannot override Rasterast, provenance, user well-being or content quality.

## Item 6 acceptance criteria

1. See → Feel → Understand → Deepen is the canonical public journey.
2. Media choice follows explanatory purpose rather than a video quota.
3. Emotion supports understanding and cannot justify fabrication/deception.
4. Fiction/dramatization/AI status remains distinguishable.
5. Plain-language explanation is available near the primary work.
6. Deeper reading and provenance are reachable without disrupting viewing.
7. Mobile and desktop layouts support the same conceptual hierarchy.
8. Sensory experience includes accessibility alternatives.
9. Age adaptation may change presentation but not source status.
10. Watch time/popularity cannot become the product's highest quality criterion.
