# Zanistarast.com — Mira Media Production Pipeline

Status: LOCKED — Roadmap item 16

## Principle

Mira may coordinate research, adaptation and original media production, but generation is never publication. Every output remains a versioned candidate until the applicable source, rights, cultural, moral, Rasterast and Müdebbir gates are satisfied.

The pipeline applies to film, animation, documentary, audio, illustration, public-language text, interactive media and future representations. Media-specific stages may be skipped only when genuinely inapplicable, never to bypass a gate.

## Pipeline

### 1. Production brief
Create a WORK-linked brief defining:
- purpose and central ethical/human question;
- intended audience;
- intended representation(s);
- source/adaptation relationship;
- culture/place/period;
- required provenance;
- known uncertainty;
- applicable scientific eligibility;
- applicable religious/moral constraints.

### 2. Source and reality research
Collect traceable source material appropriate to the work. Separate original source, historical/scientific evidence, commentary, interpretation, cultural reference and production inspiration.

Unknown facts remain unknown. Mira must not invent evidence, quotations, traditions, religious rulings or historical details to complete a production.

### 3. Rights and reuse pre-check
Before substantial adaptation, identify copyright/public-domain/license/permission constraints and intended uses. Research access is not publication permission.

If rights are unresolved, production may remain an internal candidate but cannot silently advance to public release.

### 4. Cultural/historical context research
For culturally situated works, establish the relevant people, locality, period, language, clothing, architecture, geography and material culture under Items 13–15.

### 5. Meaning and Zanistarast synthesis
Analyze the source/work without forcing every source into Zanistarast terminology. Where applicable, preserve the canonical meaning and epistemic status of Zanistarast concepts.

Cross-domain analogy remains analogy, not identity or scientific proof.

### 6. Public adaptation plan
Decide what the audience should see, feel, understand and be able to deepen into. Choose media because it serves explanation/narrative, not because video or AI generation is available.

### 7. Script / narrative / explanatory draft
Produce the appropriate script, narration, article, storyboard text or interactive flow. Distinguish quotation, paraphrase, dramatization and Mira-authored connective material in provenance.

Within this project's governing framework, the draft must remain compatible with applicable Qur'anic rulings and Prophetic ethics. Mira does not resolve uncertain religious questions by invention.

### 8. Pre-production constraints
Create structured constraints for characters, setting, costume, visual framing, sound, cultural context, audience suitability, accessibility, prohibited treatments and rights limitations.

### 9. Original media generation/production
Produce original candidate assets. Reference material may guide factual/cultural understanding but protected expression must not be copied or closely imitated.

Generation records should retain model/tool/process provenance where applicable.

### 10. Assembly and editing
Combine approved candidate elements into a coherent representation. Editing, music, sound, cropping, thumbnails and promotional derivatives are governed content and may introduce new review issues.

### 11. Representation-level review
Review the actual output rather than trusting the prompt. Applicable checks include:
- factual/source integrity;
- adaptation fidelity;
- scientific-status preservation;
- cultural/historical accuracy;
- Cultural & Moral Visual Policy;
- audience suitability;
- accessibility;
- rights/copyright/similarity;
- audio/music rights and meaning;
- misleading AI/deepfake-like presentation risk;
- thumbnail/trailer/derivative compliance.

### 12. Rasterast gate
The candidate enters the Rasterast publication gate with its exact version and review evidence. Acceptance is a publication-admission decision within this system; it is not scientific proof or independent religious authority.

Unresolved material issues return NEEDS_REVISION or ESCALATED.

### 13. Müdebbir gate
Where the governance model requires final human authority, the exact reviewed candidate and its review package go to Müdebbir. No automatic sender/publisher may bypass this decision.

### 14. Publication
Only the approved version is released. Published metadata retains work ID, representation/version, provenance, rights state, review state and applicable source links.

### 15. Post-publication monitoring
Corrections, rights changes, source corrections/retractions or discovered cultural/moral errors can trigger re-review, replacement or withdrawal. A replacement representation receives a new version and cannot inherit approval blindly.

## Gate invariants

- GENERATED != APPROVED.
- RASTERAST_ACCEPTED != SCIENTIFIC_PROOF.
- REPOSITORY_PRESENT != PUBLISHED.
- POPULAR != AUTHORITATIVE.
- LIKE/WATCH_TIME cannot bypass admission.
- UNVERIFIED != FALSE.
- ANALOGY != IDENTITY.
- UNKNOWN != permission to invent.
- A previously approved WORK does not automatically approve a new REPRESENTATION.
- A changed representation/version requires the applicable re-review.

## Failure handling

A failed stage must preserve the reason and return the candidate to the earliest stage that can correctly repair it. The system should not discard audit history or silently mutate the already reviewed version.

If a production tool/provider fails, the failure must not be converted into fabricated media, metadata or evidence.

## Automation boundary

Mira may automate research assistance, drafting, generation, checks and packaging. Critical unresolved judgments remain explicit review states.

Real-world submission/publication and other actions reserved for Müdebbir remain blocked until the required approval exists.

## Item 16 acceptance criteria

1. Media production begins from a WORK-linked brief.
2. Source research precedes generation.
3. Rights are checked before public release.
4. Cultural/historical research precedes culturally specific generation.
5. Public adaptation preserves epistemic/source distinctions.
6. Mira creates original candidates rather than copying protected expression.
7. The actual generated output is reviewed after production.
8. Visual, audio, thumbnail and derivative assets remain governed.
9. Rasterast reviews an exact version with evidence.
10. Required Müdebbir approval cannot be bypassed.
11. Only the approved version is published.
12. Corrections/rights changes can trigger re-review or withdrawal.
13. Provider/tool failures never justify fabricated evidence or media.
14. Generation, Rasterast acceptance, scientific proof and publication remain distinct states.
