## Story treatment
A person discovers an entrusted object after an ordinary social situation creates an easy opportunity to keep it. The dramatic center is not a sermon but the sequence intention → hesitation → choice → consequence → repair/trust. Social pressure and self-justification may be portrayed, but wrongdoing must not be glamorized.

## Analysis requirements

- Sociology: trust and consequences in family/community relations.
- Psychosociology: how social expectations affect individual choice and how the choice affects others.
