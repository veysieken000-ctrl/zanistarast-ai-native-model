# Zanistarast.com — Copyright and Rights Gate

Status: LOCKED — Roadmap item 18

## Principle

Research access, attribution and technical ability to copy do not create publication rights. Every source and media asset used by zanistarast.com must have a documented legal/rights basis appropriate to the intended use before public release.

Mira should prefer original production when rights for adaptation or reuse are absent, uncertain or unnecessarily restrictive. Original production must still avoid copying protected expression.

This document defines a system gate, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Material legal uncertainty remains unresolved until appropriately reviewed.

## Rights states

A source/asset/representation should support explicit states such as:

- RIGHTS_UNREVIEWED;
- RIGHTS_RESEARCH_ONLY;
- PUBLIC_DOMAIN_VERIFIED;
- OPEN_LICENSE_VERIFIED;
- PERMISSION_VERIFIED;
- LIMITED_USE_REVIEWED;
- RIGHTS_UNCLEAR;
- RIGHTS_RESTRICTED;
- RIGHTS_REJECTED;
- RIGHTS_REVIEW_EXPIRED.

Only a state whose documented basis covers the intended public use can pass the publication gate.

## Rights record

Where applicable, record:

- rights_record_id;
- source/asset/representation ID;
- rights holder/claimant if known;
- legal basis for use;
- license name/version;
- license URL/reference;
- permission evidence/reference;
- jurisdiction/territory constraints where relevant;
- allowed uses;
- prohibited/restricted uses;
- attribution requirements;
- modification/adaptation permission;
- commercial-use condition where relevant;
- share-alike/notice obligations;
- expiry/review date;
- reviewer and review timestamp;
- uncertainty/notes;
- evidence snapshot/reference.

Do not fabricate a rights holder, license or public-domain status when unknown.

## Public domain

“Old” does not automatically mean public domain. The relevant work, edition, translation, illustration, recording, photograph or restoration may have different rights.

A public-domain underlying text does not automatically make a modern translation, audiobook, film adaptation, cover image or scholarly edition public domain.

Public-domain status must therefore be attached to the specific object/version relied upon.

## Open licenses

Openly licensed material may be used only within the actual license terms. The system must preserve required attribution, notices, modification markings and other applicable obligations.

A license that permits viewing/research but not adaptation cannot be treated as adaptation permission.

## Permission

Permission-granted material should retain evidence of who granted what permission, for which material and uses, and any conditions/expiry. A vague verbal assumption is not a durable rights record.

## Quotation and limited use

Short quotation or other legally limited uses require context-specific review. The platform must not assume that citing a source makes unlimited copying lawful.

Where the legal basis depends on jurisdiction-specific exceptions, uncertainty should be escalated rather than encoded as a universal rule.

## Research versus publication

Mira may study lawful reference material to understand facts, culture, history, visual language, cinematography or storytelling techniques. Research material is not automatically reusable media.

The pipeline must distinguish:
- REFERENCE_FOR_RESEARCH;
- PERMITTED_SOURCE_MATERIAL;
- PERMITTED_REUSABLE_ASSET;
- PROHIBITED_FOR_REUSE.

## Original Mira production

When creating an original film, image, script, narration, soundtrack or other asset, Mira should synthesize ideas/facts and independently expressed production choices rather than reproduce protected expression from a specific work.

The system should guard against:
- copying frames/compositions too closely;
- reproducing distinctive protected character/art designs;
- copying protected dialogue/text beyond permitted quotation;
- imitating a protected recording;
- reusing music without rights;
- presenting another creator's asset as Mira's own;
- using “AI-generated” as a claim that rights review is unnecessary.

## Similarity review

Before publication, high-risk generated/adapted assets should undergo a similarity/originality check appropriate to the medium. A suspiciously close output returns for revision or escalation even if it was generated by Mira.

Similarity review does not itself determine legal infringement; it is a risk-control gate.

## Music, sound and voice

Music, recordings, sound effects and voice performances have their own rights/provenance. Rights to a composition do not automatically include rights to a particular recording.

Mira-created/original audio should retain production provenance. Third-party voices must not be falsely represented as real persons' authorized speech.

## Images, film and archival material

A historical fact or public event may be free to discuss while a photograph or film recording of it remains protected. The platform should use verified reusable archival material or create an original representation rather than copy merely because the depicted event is historical.

## Cultural and traditional material

Anonymous, oral or traditional material may still involve ethical, cultural, archival, performance, recording or modern-edition rights. “Folk” must not automatically be treated as rights-free.

Cultural attribution and respect obligations remain separate from copyright status.

## Religious works

Religious importance does not itself determine copyright status. Original sacred/source text, modern translations, editions, commentary, recordings, typography and media adaptations may have different rights positions and must be tracked separately where applicable.

## Withdrawal and rights change

If permission expires, a rights claim emerges, or prior rights analysis is materially corrected, affected representations must be discoverable and capable of being paused, replaced, revised or withdrawn without deleting audit history.

## Publication invariant

No recommendation score, popularity, Rasterast acceptance, scientific value, cultural importance or Müdebbir preference can substitute for a required rights basis.

Rasterast and rights review are separate gates; both must pass where applicable.

## Item 18 acceptance criteria

1. Every reusable source/asset can carry an explicit rights state.
2. Intended use must be covered before publication.
3. “Old” is not automatically public domain.
4. Rights are tracked per relevant version/edition/recording/asset.
5. Open-license obligations are preserved.
6. Permissions retain evidence, scope and conditions.
7. Citation/attribution does not equal unlimited copying permission.
8. Research/reference access is separated from reuse/publication permission.
9. Mira original production cannot rely on close copying of protected expression.
10. AI generation does not bypass rights review.
11. Music/composition/recording/voice rights are distinguishable.
12. Folk/traditional/religious material is not automatically treated as copyright-free.
13. Similarity review acts as a pre-publication risk gate.
14. Rights changes can trigger pause/revision/withdrawal.
15. Rasterast and copyright/rights approval remain independent mandatory gates.
