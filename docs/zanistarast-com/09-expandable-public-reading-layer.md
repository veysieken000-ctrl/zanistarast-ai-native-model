# Zanistarast.com — Expandable Public-Language Reading Layer

Status: LOCKED — Roadmap item 9

## Principle

Every suitable watch/work page may provide a readable public-language treatment directly beneath the primary media. The visitor should be able to understand the subject without academic training and expand into a fuller article without leaving the work context.

This layer is an adaptation/explanation, not a license to change the source meaning, evidence status or ethical boundaries.

## Default collapsed view

Directly beneath the media/actions, show a compact block containing:

- a short plain-language summary;
- the central human/ethical question;
- essential context needed not to misunderstand the work;
- an explicit “Read / Continue reading” control.

The collapsed state should be useful on its own and must not use clickbait truncation that conceals essential qualifications.

## Expanded view

When opened in place, the reading layer may contain:

- fuller public-language narrative/explanation;
- sections with clear headings;
- examples and consequences;
- cultural/historical context;
- distinctions between story, interpretation and evidence;
- important uncertainty/caveats;
- a compact “What is the source?” path;
- links to deeper provenance/scientific material where eligible.

Opening/closing must not reset video playback or navigate away unless the visitor explicitly follows a source link.

## Writing standard

Public-language text should:

- prefer short, concrete sentences and familiar vocabulary;
- explain necessary specialist terms when first used;
- use examples and narrative where helpful;
- avoid unnecessary academic density;
- avoid patronizing or childish language for adults;
- preserve nuance when simplification would become false;
- never turn a moral lesson into a fabricated scientific claim.

Zanistarast terminology may appear where it genuinely clarifies the deeper synthesis, but the visitor must not need specialist vocabulary to grasp the public explanation.

## Source and adaptation labeling

The reading layer must identify its relationship to the source: public-language adaptation, retelling, commentary, dramatization companion, scientific-public summary or another accurate type.

Direct quotation, paraphrase and Mira-generated connective explanation must remain distinguishable in the underlying provenance model. Copyright limits and quotation rules remain governed by the legal gate.

## Religious material

Where Qur'anic material, Prophetic ethics, hadith/sirah, Risale-i Nur or later interpretation is discussed, the public-language layer must not silently present commentary/adaptation as the original source.

Within the governing framework of this project, the explanation must remain compatible with the applicable Qur'anic rulings and Prophetic ethics. Mira must not invent a religious ruling to make the explanation simpler; unresolved interpretation/source questions go to Rasterast and, where required, Müdebbir.

## Scientific material

For eligible scientific works, the reading layer must preserve distinctions such as result, interpretation, hypothesis, analogy, limitation and counterevidence. “Easy to read” must never mean “more certain than the source.”

A visible path to the verified .org/publication record should be available when applicable.

## Reading UX

- expanded/collapsed state should be obvious;
- the control must be keyboard and screen-reader operable;
- headings and paragraphs use semantic HTML;
- long text should remain readable on mobile;
- expansion should avoid large layout jumps where practical;
- browser back/forward behavior should remain predictable;
- deep linking to a reading section may be supported later.

## Audio reading

A future “listen to this text” option may narrate the public-language article. Audio narration is another representation/derivative and must use the same approved text/version rather than silently generating a different explanation.

## Item 9 acceptance criteria

1. A compact useful summary appears beneath suitable primary media.
2. Full public-language reading expands/collapses in place.
3. Video/playback context is preserved during expansion.
4. Essential caveats cannot be hidden merely to increase clicks.
5. Plain language simplifies expression, not epistemic status.
6. Adaptation/source relationship is explicit.
7. Religious commentary is not confused with original source text.
8. Scientific uncertainty and counterevidence remain representable.
9. Source/.org provenance is reachable where applicable.
10. Reading controls and text structure are accessible and mobile-friendly.
11. Future audio reading must narrate the approved version faithfully.
