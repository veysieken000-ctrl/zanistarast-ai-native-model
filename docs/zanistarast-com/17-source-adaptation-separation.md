# Zanistarast.com — Source, Adaptation and Interpretation Separation

Status: LOCKED — Roadmap item 17

## Principle

zanistarast.com must never blur an original source with Mira's adaptation, a Zanistarast interpretation, a dramatization, a translation or a public explanation.

A visitor should be able to tell what comes from the source, what was transformed, what was added for presentation, and what is later analysis.

## Content relationship types

The provenance model must support explicit relationships such as:

- ORIGINAL_SOURCE — the underlying source record/work;
- DIRECT_QUOTATION — exact permitted quotation from a source;
- TRANSLATION — a translation of source text;
- PARAPHRASE — meaning restated without claiming verbatim wording;
- RETELLING — narrative retelling that preserves the source relationship;
- ADAPTATION — transformed into another form or setting;
- DRAMATIZATION — scenes/dialogue created to represent or explore source material;
- COMMENTARY — explanatory commentary about a source;
- ZANISTARAST_INTERPRETATION — analysis/synthesis produced through the Zanistarast framework;
- PUBLIC_EXPLANATION — accessible explanation for a general audience;
- INSPIRED_BY — an original work influenced by a source without claiming to reproduce it;
- FICTIONAL_RECONSTRUCTION — invented connective material used in a clearly disclosed reconstruction.

These relationships are not interchangeable.

## Attribution rule

The system must never attribute Mira-written dialogue, narration, examples or conclusions to an original author/source unless they are genuinely present there and appropriately evidenced.

For example, a Mira-created scene inspired by an author's work remains a Mira adaptation/dramatization; it must not be presented as a lost or direct scene by that author.

The same rule applies to historical figures, communities, religious sources and scientific authors.

## Quotations

Quotation must remain traceable to the source and comply with rights/copyright limits. A translation of a quotation must be identified as a translation when that distinction matters.

Mira must not reconstruct an uncertain quotation from memory and present it in quotation marks as exact text.

## Religious sources

Qur'anic text, translation/meaning, hadith/sirah material, commentary, Risale-i Nur material and Zanistarast/Mira interpretation must remain distinguishable.

Within this project's governing framework, adaptations must remain compatible with applicable Qur'anic rulings and Prophetic ethics, but compatibility does not turn a later explanation into revelation, hadith or original religious text.

If authenticity, interpretation or ruling is unresolved, Mira must preserve the uncertainty and route the issue through applicable Rasterast/Müdebbir review rather than inventing certainty.

## Scientific sources

A scientific paper's reported result, authors' interpretation, later commentary and Zanistarast synthesis must remain separately attributable.

A public adaptation cannot:
- strengthen a tentative result into certainty;
- hide a material limitation/counterevidence;
- present Zanistarast interpretation as the source authors' conclusion;
- turn analogy into scientific identity;
- invent a DOI, publication status or evidence.

## Historical and cultural sources

A retelling may add connective narrative material when appropriate, but invented dialogue/events/customs must not be attributed as documented history.

For anonymous/oral traditions, provenance uncertainty and known variants should remain representable.

## Adaptation setting

Changing the visual setting does not change source origin. A universal story visually adapted in Kurdistan, for example, records its adaptation setting separately from its source provenance.

A known story from another people retains that people's source identity under Items 13–14.

## User-facing disclosure

The work page should be able to present a concise relationship label such as:

“Adapted from …”
“Retelling of …”
“Inspired by …”
“Public explanation of …”
“Zanistarast interpretation based on …”

The detailed provenance view may expose the full relationship graph without overloading the ordinary viewing experience.

## Versioning

Every adaptation/translation/commentary is versioned independently from its source. Updating an adaptation never mutates the source record.

If a source is corrected, retracted, reclassified or its rights status changes, dependent works must be discoverable for review.

## Rights boundary

Source attribution does not grant adaptation rights. Public-domain, license, permission, quotation exceptions and other legal bases remain the responsibility of the rights gate.

Likewise, transformation by AI does not automatically make copying lawful or original.

## Item 17 acceptance criteria

1. Original source and derivative work are separate records.
2. Translation, paraphrase, retelling, adaptation, dramatization, commentary, interpretation and inspiration have explicit semantics.
3. Mira-generated material cannot be falsely attributed to a source/author.
4. Exact quotations remain traceable and rights-aware.
5. Religious source text and later commentary/interpretation remain distinct.
6. Scientific source results and Zanistarast synthesis remain distinct.
7. Invented historical dialogue/events cannot masquerade as documented history.
8. Adaptation setting cannot rewrite source culture/origin.
9. Public pages can show a simple source-relationship label.
10. Derivatives are independently versioned.
11. Source corrections/retractions/rights changes can propagate to dependent-work review.
12. Attribution alone never substitutes for copyright permission.
