# Zanistarast.com — Mission-First Advertising Roadmap

Status: LOCKED DIRECTION — implementation follows Package H.

## 1. Purpose hierarchy

Zanistarast.com is not optimized primarily for advertising revenue. Product decisions follow this order:

1. teach and make understandable the Zanistarast scientific synthesis without overstating epistemic status;
2. support ethical education around goodness, truthfulness, honesty, justice, mercy, responsibility and other admitted virtues;
3. communicate the Newroza Kawa civilization direction through governed public media;
4. protect the viewer's attention, agency, dignity and trust;
5. preserve scientific, source, cultural, religious, legal and copyright boundaries;
6. only then permit sustainable revenue, including advertising, when it does not override the preceding purposes.

Revenue, advertiser demand, engagement, likes, watch time or campaign spend never grant publication/admission authority.

## 2. Advertising is subordinate infrastructure

Advertising may support the platform financially, but it is not the editorial center. The platform may refuse, pause, withdraw or limit a campaign even when doing so reduces revenue.

Advertising must not:
- displace education or admitted works from discovery;
- buy placement in "Yeni Eklenenler";
- alter scientific or Rasterast status;
- force excessive interruption to meet a campaign quota;
- imitate editorial/scientific content without clear disclosure;
- exploit children or vulnerable audiences;
- use deceptive, coercive, hidden or subliminal persuasion.

## 3. Mira-created advertising by default

Preferred external-ad workflow:

advertiser brief + factual claims + product/service assets + rights evidence
→ Mira research and claim verification
→ rights/legal review
→ hidden/deceptive/subliminal-technique review
→ cultural and moral visual review
→ Zanistarast conceptual compatibility review
→ Hebûn / Zanabûn / Mabûn / Rabûn trace where applicable and canonically defined
→ Rasterast review
→ Mira creates an original Zanistarast-hosted ad representation
→ similarity/copyright review
→ exact-version review package
→ Müdebbir decision when required
→ admitted campaign pool.

The advertiser's supplied finished video is treated as untrusted source material, not automatically as the public creative. Where an external asset must be used, it receives the same exact-version gates and can be rejected.

Mira must not invent meanings for Hebûn, Zanabûn, Mabûn, Rabûn or Rasterast. Unresolved conceptual status remains explicit and is escalated rather than guessed.

## 4. Anti-manipulation rule

No admitted advertisement may intentionally contain subliminal frames/audio, hidden calls to action, disguised sponsorship, deceptive edits, misleading scarcity, false claims, dark-pattern interaction, or other techniques intended to bypass conscious viewer judgment.

Automated analysis is defense-in-depth, not a guarantee that every manipulation can be detected. High-risk or ambiguous material remains blocked pending human review.

## 5. Exact-version evidence

Every campaign has:
- campaign ID and version;
- advertiser/sponsor identity;
- start/end dates;
- approved claims and evidence;
- source asset hashes/identities where available;
- rights/license evidence;
- generated creative ID/version;
- target link;
- required disclosures;
- review results;
- unresolved items;
- admission/withdrawal state.

Changing video, audio, voice-over, text, link, claims, thumbnail or material targeting creates a new reviewable version. Approval does not silently transfer.

## 6. Viewer-first frequency policy

Initial conservative policy:
- skip may become available after 5 seconds where the format permits;
- the same interruptive campaign appears at most once per session;
- at least 15 minutes OR 3 consumed works between interruptive promotions, whichever produces fewer interruptions;
- at most 3 interruptive promotions per session;
- no mid-roll interruption in short works;
- long works use only defined natural break points;
- playback failure, campaign exhaustion or ad-service failure immediately yields to the requested work;
- internal Zanistarast announcements remain discoverable through the non-interruptive Tanıtımlar shelf;
- newly admitted works remain discoverable through Yeni Eklenenler independently of advertising.

These are maximum interruption limits, not delivery targets. Mira may show fewer ads.

## 7. Campaign scheduling objective

Mira Ad Scheduler optimizes in this order:
1. eligibility and exact-version governance;
2. viewer interruption limits;
3. relevance without sensitive-trait inference;
4. fair rotation and repeat suppression;
5. campaign pacing within remaining eligible opportunities.

A purchased impression target never authorizes exceeding viewer limits. If a campaign cannot be fulfilled safely within its term, the system reports under-delivery instead of increasing pressure on viewers.

## 8. Withdrawal and audit

Any later rights issue, factual correction, Rasterast conflict, cultural/moral issue, deceptive-technique finding or Müdebbir withdrawal immediately removes the exact campaign version from eligible delivery. Historical audit records remain separate from public delivery.

H3 — Mira creative-generation handoff and source ledger.
H4 — anti-manipulation/static media inspection boundary.
H5 — scheduler with viewer-first frequency caps.

H8 — audit, withdrawal and campaign expiry.
H9 — DEMO end-to-end campaign.
H10 — only after governance/privacy/legal/deployment readiness, optional external advertiser intake and billing integration.

## Acceptance principle

The advertising system is successful when it can finance part of the platform without becoming the platform's purpose, without weakening Zanistarast/Newroza Kawa governance, and without turning the viewer's attention into the highest-priority resource.
