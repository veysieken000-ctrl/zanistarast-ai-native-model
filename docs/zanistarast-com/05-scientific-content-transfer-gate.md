# Zanistarast.com — Scientific Content Transfer Gate

Status: LOCKED — Roadmap item 5

## Principle

Material present on zanistarast.org, in the repository, or in Mira's working knowledge is not automatically eligible for public adaptation on zanistarast.com.

The .org → .com boundary is a controlled scientific transfer gate. It prevents drafts, working notes, unverified synthesis and publication candidates from being presented to the public as established or published science.

## Scientific lifecycle

A Zanistarast scientific item may move through stages such as:

WORKING_MATERIAL → SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW → TARGET_SELECTED → TARGET_ADAPTED → DISTINCTIVENESS_AND_EVIDENCE_REVIEW → RASTERAST_REVIEW → PUBLICATION_READY → MÜDEBBIR_DECISION → SUBMITTED/PUBLISHED_OR_REPOSITORY_RECORD → VERIFIED_PUBLICATION_RECORD → COM_ADAPTATION_ELIGIBLE

The exact scholarly workflow may evolve, but .com must consume an explicit eligibility result rather than infer readiness from file location, filename, repository presence or a generic status label.

## Current working articles

Current site/repository articles that have not completed the required target-journal/publication-readiness process remain working/reference material for Mira. They must not receive public-facing film, animation, documentary, audio or other media enrichment merely because they are available.

## Eligibility record

Before scientific material is adapted for .com, the system should be able to identify:

- source work/article ID;
- title and authorship;
- current scientific status;
- target publication/repository where applicable;
- version being adapted;
- Rasterast/review status;
- Müdebbir decision where required;
- DOI or other legitimate persistent/publication identifier where one actually exists;
- canonical .org/publication source;
- correction/retraction status;
- date/version of eligibility decision.

No DOI, identifier, acceptance or publication state may be invented.

## Persistent identifiers

A DOI or other persistent identifier strengthens provenance when legitimately issued, but an identifier alone does not prove scientific truth or make every item suitable for .com. Conversely, the system must not fabricate or prematurely reserve an identifier merely to satisfy this gate.

## Adaptation contract

Once eligible, Mira may create a public-language treatment and suitable media. The adaptation must:

1. preserve the scientific claim and evidence boundary;
2. distinguish established result, interpretation, hypothesis, analogy and uncertainty;
3. retain meaningful counterevidence/caveats;
4. link or identify the verified scientific source;
5. identify itself as an adaptation rather than the journal/repository version;
6. avoid adding scientific claims that are absent from the source;
7. be rechecked if the underlying scientific record is corrected, withdrawn or materially updated.

## Publication-ready versus published

PUBLICATION_READY and PUBLISHED are distinct. A publication-ready work may be used for .com only if the governing Zanistarast policy explicitly marks it COM_ADAPTATION_ELIGIBLE after required review/Müdebbir decision. The public interface must accurately label its status and must not call it published when it is not.

Where the policy requires a verified publication/persistent-identifier record before public adaptation, that stricter requirement controls.

## Version and correction propagation

A .com adaptation must retain a machine-readable relationship to the exact scientific source/version it adapts. If that source is corrected, retracted, superseded or loses eligibility, the .com item must be reviewable and capable of correction, relabeling, suspension or withdrawal.

## No automatic promotion

The following never establish .com eligibility by themselves:

- presence on zanistarast.org;
- presence in GitHub/repository memory;
- a high retrieval score;
- Mira confidence;
- popularity or user demand;
- existence of media assets;
- existence of a DOI without the required review state.

## Item 5 acceptance criteria

1. Draft/current working articles cannot automatically flow to .com.
2. .com consumes explicit scientific eligibility rather than guessing it.
3. Publication-ready and published states remain distinct.
4. DOI/persistent identifiers are never invented and never equal truth.
5. Adaptations preserve claims, uncertainty, caveats and provenance.
6. The exact adapted source/version is traceable.
7. Corrections/retractions can propagate to .com.
8. Mira confidence, popularity and repository presence cannot bypass the gate.
9. Media enrichment starts only after the applicable scientific eligibility requirements are satisfied.
