# Zanistarast.com — Source Universe and Classification

Status: LOCKED — Roadmap item 4

## Principle

Zanistarast.com may draw ethical, educational and cultural material from a broad human heritage. Admission is never based on fame alone. Every candidate source retains its identity, provenance, legal status and epistemic/source type, and remains subject to the Rasterast and downstream publication gates.

## Source families

The source registry must be able to distinguish at least:

- Qur'an and explicitly identified Islamic primary/religious material;
- Prophetic ethics and properly sourced hadith/sirah material;
- Risale-i Nur works and clearly identified passages/themes;
- Zanistarast scientific and conceptual works;
- peer-reviewed or otherwise identifiable scientific research;
- philosophy and ethical thought;
- literature, novels, short stories, tales, fables and poetry;
- folk/oral/anonymous traditions;
- historical records and biographies;
- cultural traditions and documented social practices;
- public-domain works;
- openly licensed or permission-granted works;
- original Zanistarast/Mira productions;
- user/community-submitted candidate material, if enabled later.

These categories describe provenance/type; they do not declare equal authority, truth status or evidentiary weight.

## World heritage

Suitable works and stories from Kurdish and other peoples may be considered: classics, moral stories, folk tales, historical examples, thinkers and writers such as Tolstoy, Dostoevsky, Confucius, and many others. Inclusion depends on the actual work/content, not the author's reputation as a blanket approval.

A work from another people remains attributed to that people/source where known. It is not relabeled as Kurdish, Islamic or Zanistarast merely because it is adapted for this platform.

## Source record

Each candidate should be capable of carrying:

- canonical/known title;
- creator/author/collector where known;
- people/culture and geographic context where known;
- original language and date/period where known;
- source family/type;
- edition/publication/URL/identifier as applicable;
- excerpt or proposition actually relied upon;
- public-domain, license, permission or copyright-review status;
- adaptation status;
- scientific/epistemic status where relevant;
- Rasterast state;
- provenance notes and uncertainty.

Unknown information must be recorded as unknown rather than invented.

## Anonymous and oral material

Anonymous, folk and oral traditions require provenance care. The platform should distinguish “author unknown” from “source unknown.” Where a story is associated with a people or region but authorship is unknown, that cultural provenance should be retained with an appropriate uncertainty note.

## Adaptation rule

Source material may inspire a public-language article, film, animation, audio work or other media only when the adaptation process preserves the source relationship. The adaptation must identify whether it is a translation, retelling, dramatization, commentary, synthesis, inspired original work or other transformation.

A Mira-created work based on multiple references is a new adaptation/production; it must not masquerade as the verbatim work of any referenced author.

## Scientific-source rule

Scientific sources require claim-level care. A study, paper or Zanistarast publication may support only what its evidence/status supports. Public storytelling must not convert a hypothesis, analogy, preliminary result or interpretation into an established fact.

Eligibility of Zanistarast.org scientific material for .com remains governed by the publication-readiness boundary in roadmap item 5.

## Religious-source rule

Religious sources must be cited/identified according to their actual source and type. Qur'an, hadith/sirah material, Risale-i Nur, later commentary and Zanistarast interpretation must remain distinguishable. Adaptation or commentary must not be presented as the original sacred/source text.

## Legal boundary

Discovery of a valuable story or work does not itself grant reproduction rights. Full-text, audiovisual, translation and adaptation rights are separate questions. Copyright/public-domain/license/permission review is mandatory under the dedicated legal roadmap gate before publication.

## Item 4 acceptance criteria

1. The source universe supports many peoples, periods and source types.
2. Source type never automatically implies equal authority or evidence.
3. Individual works are reviewed rather than blanket-approving famous authors.
4. Cultural and authorial provenance is preserved.
5. Anonymous does not automatically mean provenance-free.
6. Adaptation type is explicit and Mira output cannot impersonate its sources.
7. Scientific claims retain their evidence/status limits.
8. Religious source, commentary and Zanistarast interpretation remain distinguishable.
9. Unknown provenance is never fabricated.
10. Copyright/license permission remains a separate mandatory publication gate.
