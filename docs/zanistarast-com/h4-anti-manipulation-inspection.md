# H4 — Anti-Manipulation Inspection Boundary

Status: COMPLETE (code-side)
Date: 2026-09-26

## Boundary

No advertising creative is cleared merely because it is supplied by an advertiser, generated by Mira, technically playable, or commercially purchased.

The exact creative version must be inspectable and its required review evidence must correspond to that same version.

## Inspection states

- PASS — required inspection inputs are present and no blocking signal is recorded.
- REVIEW — inspection is incomplete; public delivery is not authorized.
- BLOCK — identity/version integrity failed or a prohibited manipulation signal is recorded.

## Required inspection surfaces

Depending on representation type, inspection covers:
- visible text and overlays;
- video frames and transitions;
- audio/voice;
- metadata and declared destination;
- sponsorship disclosure;
- interaction behavior.

Video requires frame review. Video/audio requires audio review. Metadata review is required. Missing review is fail-closed to REVIEW rather than silently PASS.

## Blocking manipulation signals

The machine-readable boundary currently blocks:
- HIDDEN_MESSAGE;
- SYSTEM_WARNING_IMPERSONATION;
- UNDISCLOSED_SPONSORSHIP;
- DECEPTIVE_COUNTDOWN;
- FORCED_CLICK;
- SENSORY_MANIPULATION.

This list is extensible. Detection is evidence for governance; absence of a detected signal is not proof that a creative is ethically safe.

## Authority rule

The inspection helper has `automatedClearance:false`. It cannot self-authorize publication. A PASS may produce exact-version anti-manipulation review evidence for the wider campaign admission chain, but human/governed review remains required where the policy requires it.

## Verification

Automated smoke tests cover PASS, incomplete REVIEW, blocking hidden-message/system-impersonation signals, exact-version evidence and refusal to emit PASS evidence from an incomplete inspection.
