# Zanistarast.com — Kurdistan Default Visual Context

Status: LOCKED — Roadmap item 14

## Principle

Culturally identified works retain their own people, geography, history and visual context under Item 13. The Kurdistan default defined here applies only when the work is genuinely unassigned/anonymous/universal, or when an eligible Islamic or Risale-i Nur adaptation has been deliberately designated for this visual treatment.

It is a presentation context, not a claim that the underlying source originated in Kurdistan.

## Eligible use

The default may be considered for:

- anonymous or universal moral stories with no binding cultural setting;
- newly created Mira stories without another required cultural setting;
- eligible Qur'anic/Prophetic-ethics educational adaptations when a human visual setting is appropriate and approved;
- eligible Risale-i Nur public adaptations when designated for this treatment;
- other explicitly approved works whose provenance is not misrepresented by the choice.

## Non-eligible override

The default must not replace:

- a known source culture;
- the documented setting of a historical event;
- the identity of a real historical person;
- a source's materially significant geography, architecture, clothing or social setting;
- another people's tale merely because the platform prefers Kurdistan imagery.

Historical and provenance truth take priority.

## Visual vocabulary

Where applicable and adequately researched, the Kurdistan visual context may draw from:

- appropriate Kurdistan landscapes and settlements;
- regionally and historically suitable architecture;
- local materials and domestic spaces;
- traditional/local clothing appropriate to region, period, age and social context;
- agriculture, crafts and everyday material culture;
- season, terrain and environment;
- language/signage details when contextually appropriate.

“Kurdish” must not be reduced to one costume, one landscape or one province. Regional and historical variation must remain representable.

## Research requirement

Mira must research the intended locality/period before generating culturally specific visual assets. If a precise regional claim cannot be supported, the representation should remain appropriately broad rather than inventing false specificity.

Modern tourism imagery, staged costume photography and contemporary reconstructions are references requiring contextual caution; they are not automatic evidence for historical appearance.

## Religious-source integrity

Using Kurdistan as a visual setting for an Islamic or Risale-i Nur adaptation must not:

- alter the original wording or source attribution;
- imply that Qur'anic revelation or a Prophetic event occurred in Kurdistan when it did not;
- relocate a documented sacred/historical event;
- turn later commentary into Qur'anic text, hadith or established history;
- invent a religious ruling or scene merely to fit the visual setting.

Where depiction itself raises a religious or interpretive concern, the representation remains pending and goes through the applicable Rasterast/Müdebbir review.

## Prophetic and sacred representation

This default is not authorization to visually depict Prophets, sacred events or religious figures in ways that conflict with the project's governing Qur'anic-ruling and Prophetic-ethics constraints. Such cases require their own explicit representation policy/review before production.

## Moral and visual boundary

All generated imagery remains subject to the later Cultural & Moral Visual Policy. Clothing, framing, posture, dialogue and scene construction must satisfy the project's applicable edep/haya/terbiye requirements as well as the governing Qur'anic-ruling and Prophetic-ethics publication boundary.

The visual default cannot be used to romanticize, sexualize or stereotype Kurdish people.

## Disclosure and provenance

The system should distinguish:

- SOURCE CULTURE/ORIGIN;
- STORY SETTING;
- ADAPTATION VISUAL SETTING;
- PRODUCTION CULTURAL REFERENCES.

For example, a universal anonymous tale visually adapted in Kurdistan must retain “anonymous/unassigned source” while separately recording “Kurdistan visual adaptation.” The adaptation must not rewrite source provenance.

## Originality and rights

Researching Kurdish clothing, architecture, landscape, photography or cinema does not permit copying a protected photograph, illustration, costume design, film frame, soundtrack or other protected expression.

Mira should synthesize researched factual/cultural characteristics into an original production and preserve rights/provenance records.

## Item 14 acceptance criteria

1. Kurdistan is a default visual context only for eligible unassigned/designated works.
2. Known source cultures and real historical settings cannot be overwritten.
3. Kurdistan is represented with regional/historical variation rather than a single stereotype.
4. Geography, architecture, clothing and material culture require research.
5. Unsupported regional specificity is not fabricated.
6. Source origin and adaptation setting remain separate metadata.
7. Islamic/Risale-i Nur adaptation does not relocate or falsify documented sacred/history claims.
8. Mira cannot invent religious rulings or source material to fit a visual setting.
9. Sensitive sacred/Prophetic representation requires explicit later policy/review.
10. Qur'anic-ruling, Prophetic-ethics and edep/haya constraints remain binding.
11. Cultural research does not grant copyright permission to copy protected expression.
12. The resulting media must remain an original production with traceable provenance.
