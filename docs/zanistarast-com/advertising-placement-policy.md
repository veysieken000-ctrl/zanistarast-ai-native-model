# Advertising Placement Policy

Status: ACCEPTED BY MÜDEBBIR
Decision date: 2026-09-26

This record makes the accepted Zanistarast.com advertising placement decision reusable system knowledge.

## Allowed placement families

1. PRE_ROLL — interruptive video promotion before requested media; skip behavior follows the governed playback policy.
2. NATURAL_BREAK_MID_ROLL — only for sufficiently long works and only at an authored/natural break point. Short works must not receive mid-roll.
3. FEED_SPONSORED_CARD — clearly disclosed sponsored card inside discovery/home feeds; must not masquerade as organic content.
4. WATCH_SIDE_OR_BELOW_CARD — non-interruptive sponsored placement beside the player on wide layouts or below it on narrow/mobile layouts.
5. SHORT_FORM_BETWEEN_ITEMS — for a future short-form stream; placed between works and immediately dismissible/swipeable.
6. QUIET_SPONSORED_BANNER — non-interruptive, clearly disclosed static/quiet placement; no surprise autoplay audio.
7. INTERNAL_ANNOUNCEMENT — Zanistarast publication/work announcement. This is not an external advertiser entitlement and its non-interruptive shelf remains separately discoverable.

## Placement selection

Advertisers provide an admitted campaign and governed creative inputs; they do not gain an unrestricted right to force a placement. Mira selects among permitted placements based on creative format, device/layout, requested work, viewer-frequency policy, campaign entitlement and governance state.

A campaign may have exact-version derivatives adapted for different placements, but each material creative version remains subject to the applicable exact-version review rules.

## Viewer protection

Do not use forced large pop-ups or long unskippable advertising as a default Zanistarast format. Interruptive placements remain subordinate to viewer-first frequency caps. Rapid navigation must not cause an advertisement on every click. Natural-break mid-roll must never be synthesized at arbitrary timestamps merely to satisfy delivery quota.

## Accounting

Every external delivery event must be attributable to campaign ID/version, creative ID/version and placement type. Reporting must support delivered and remaining entitlement by placement as well as campaign total. Internal announcements do not consume external advertiser entitlement.

## Governance

Placement eligibility never bypasses rights, claims, anti-manipulation, cultural/moral, Rasterast or Müdebbir gates. Paid status, quota pressure or advertiser preference cannot override these gates.
