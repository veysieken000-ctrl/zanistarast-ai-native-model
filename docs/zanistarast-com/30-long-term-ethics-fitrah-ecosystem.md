# Zanistarast.com — Long-Term Ethics and Fitrah Public Media Ecosystem

Status: LOCKED — Roadmap item 30

## Principle

zanistarast.com is intended to grow beyond a catalogue of videos or articles into a public media-and-learning ecosystem centered on ethics, fitrah, meaningful knowledge and responsible human action.

The long-term product must remain easy to enter while allowing a person to move from seeing and feeling to understanding, learning, reflection and deeper source exploration.

The governing public journey remains:

**Gör → Hisset → Anla → Derinleş**

Growth must preserve this purpose rather than turn the platform into an attention-maximization network.

## Ecosystem, not a single format

The central object remains WORK. A work may be represented through one or more approved forms:

- film and short film;
- documentary;
- animation/cartoon;
- story and tale;
- audio/narration;
- public-language reading;
- illustration/infographic;
- map/timeline;
- interactive learning experience;
- accessible derivatives;
- future media forms.

No single medium defines Zanistarast.com.

## Public entry, deeper layers

The first layer should remain understandable to an ordinary visitor.

A person may then move deeper through:
1. the work itself;
2. concise explanation;
3. “Oku / Konuyu Anla”;
4. source/provenance;
5. related works and values;
6. eligible scientific/source material;
7. deeper Zanistarast conceptual material where relevant.

The user is not forced to learn specialist terminology before receiving value.

## Ethics and fitrah orientation

Discovery and learning may organize around humanly understandable themes such as:

- mercy;
- justice;
- truthfulness;
- trust;
- modesty and manners;
- patience;
- responsibility;
- courage;
- family;
- friendship;
- forgiveness;
- care;
- discipline/upbringing;
- intention, will, choice and consequence.

These themes are discovery/learning concepts, not automatic claims that every tagged work has equal authority or evidentiary status.

## Governing religious boundary

Within the project's declared framework, the ecosystem must not publish material contrary to applicable Qur'anic rulings and Prophetic ethics.

This boundary applies to:
- source selection;
- adaptation;
- script/dialogue;
- character treatment;
- clothing;
- camera/framing;
- imagery;
- audio/music decisions;
- thumbnails/trailers;
- recommendation packaging;
- educational derivatives.

Mira must not invent religious rulings to resolve uncertainty. Consequential unresolved cases remain explicit and follow the applicable review/escalation path.

## Source plurality without authority collapse

The ecosystem may responsibly draw from distinct source classes, including where legally and contextually appropriate:

- Qur'anic sources;
- properly sourced Prophetic ethics, hadith and sirah material;
- Risale-i Nur;
- Zanistarast works;
- scientific research;
- philosophy and ethical thought;
- literature;
- historical records and biographies;
- folk/oral traditions;
- cultural traditions;
- public-domain, openly licensed or permission-granted works.

These source classes are not collapsed into one authority type. Their provenance, interpretation and evidentiary status remain visible.

## Learning model

The ecosystem should support learning through multiple paths:

### Narrative learning
People encounter choices, motives and consequences through credible stories.

### Explanatory learning
Public-language explanations make difficult subjects understandable without falsifying them.

### Source learning
Visitors can inspect where claims, stories and adaptations come from.

### Comparative learning
Where appropriate, multiple traditions, historical cases or scientific findings may be compared without erasing their differences.

### Reflective learning
Prompts/questions may help a person think about intention, responsibility, relationships and consequences without coercive moral manipulation.

## Collections and pathways

Over time, approved works may be assembled into governed learning pathways such as:
- a value/theme pathway;
- family/age-appropriate pathway;
- culture/history pathway;
- story-to-source pathway;
- public explanation to scientific-source pathway;
- curated Newroza Kawa public-learning pathway.

Collections do not grant approval to their members; every included work remains independently eligible.

## Relationship to zanistarast.org

zanistarast.org remains the scientific/academic/verification publication layer.

zanistarast.com remains the public media/learning layer.

Eligible .org work may inform .com adaptations only through the scientific transfer gate. The .com experience must not make a working repository article look peer-reviewed/published merely because it exists on .org or GitHub.

Conversely, popularity on .com is not scientific validation for .org.

## Mira's long-term role

Mira may become a coordinated public-production system able to:
- research;
- synthesize;
- adapt;
- draft;
- create candidate media;
- prepare translations;
- create accessibility derivatives;
- check continuity/similarity;
- assemble review evidence;
- monitor corrections and dependencies.

Mira remains constrained by provenance, rights, cultural, moral, Rasterast and Müdebbir gates. Increased capability does not create increased authority.

## Community/user participation

Future participation may include:
- likes and saves;
- reports/corrections;
- learning progress;
- carefully governed feedback;
- possibly submissions or contributions if a future policy defines them.

User-generated or submitted material must never enter the public corpus without applicable admission/review. Popularity is not admission.

The platform should avoid designing social status, outrage or virality as its core incentive.

## Recommendation philosophy

Recommendation should help people find relevant approved material, not maximize compulsive consumption.

Signals may improve relevance, but the system should preserve:
- user agency;
- thematic diversity;
- age/audience suitability;
- language preference;
- source transparency;
- governance eligibility.

Infinite engagement is not the mission.

## Children and families

Children/family experiences require stronger safeguards and age-appropriate presentation.

The product should avoid:
- manipulative guilt/fear;
- addictive reward loops;
- humiliation;
- inappropriate visual framing;
- disguised advertising;
- uncontrolled recommendation drift.

Parental/family-oriented discovery may be introduced later without treating adults and children as the same audience.

## Cultural breadth

The ecosystem may represent many peoples and traditions while preserving cultural provenance and internal diversity.

Known cultural/historical settings are not overwritten by the Kurdistan default. The Kurdistan visual default applies only to the eligible contexts defined in Item 14.

Cultural representation must not become caricature or aesthetic extraction.

## Multilingual future

Kurmancî/Kurdish remains first-class while the architecture supports additional Kurdish varieties/scripts and world languages.

Language growth should prioritize reviewed quality over machine-generated volume.

Subtitles, dubbing, narration and translated readings remain versioned governed representations.

## Institutional future

If the platform later supports schools, universities, libraries, families, researchers or cultural institutions, public and institutional modes should reuse the same canonical works and provenance rather than create contradictory copies.

Institutional features may add:
- curated learning sets;
- educator guides;
- citation/source exports;
- accessibility packages;
- research/source navigation;
- controlled classroom/family pathways.

Such expansion does not weaken publication gates.

## Sustainability

Long-term sustainability must not require violating the mission.

Future funding/business choices must not allow:
- advertisers/sponsors to alter Rasterast eligibility;
- paid placement to masquerade as epistemic authority;
- engagement targets to override moral/content gates;
- sponsors to suppress corrections;
- commercial pressure to mass-publish weak AI content.

Any future commercial/sponsorship model requires its own transparent policy before implementation.

## Metrics

Useful product metrics may include:
- successful playback/read completion;
- search success;
- accessibility success;
- correction response time;
- source-detail usage;
- learning-path continuation;
- performance/reliability;
- user-controlled saves/likes.

Metrics must be interpreted carefully. Watch time, likes or growth do not measure truth, morality or scientific validity.

## Evolution discipline

New features should answer:
1. Does this help people see, feel, understand or deepen?
2. Does it preserve human agency?
3. Does it preserve source/epistemic distinctions?
4. Can it operate without weakening rights, cultural, moral, Rasterast or Müdebbir gates?
5. Does it strengthen meaningful public learning rather than merely attention capture?

If not, the feature does not belong merely because another media platform has it.

## Item 30 acceptance criteria

1. .com is defined as a public media-and-learning ecosystem, not a video clone.
2. WORK remains medium-independent.
3. Gör → Hisset → Anla → Derinleş remains the public journey.
4. Ordinary users can enter without specialist terminology.
5. Ethics/fitrah themes organize discovery without becoming authority scores.
6. Qur'anic-ruling/Prophetic-ethics boundary applies across the ecosystem.
7. Distinct source classes retain distinct provenance/authority/status.
8. Narrative, explanatory, source, comparative and reflective learning are supported.
9. .org and .com retain separate scientific/public responsibilities.
10. Mira capability never becomes self-granted publication authority.
11. Community participation cannot bypass admission/review.
12. Recommendation optimizes relevance within approved content, not compulsive engagement.
13. Children/family experiences receive stronger safeguards.
14. Cultural breadth and multilingual growth preserve provenance and review quality.
15. Future institutional use reuses canonical governed works.
16. Future commercial pressure cannot override governance.
17. Metrics are never treated as truth/morality/scientific-validity scores.
18. New features must serve the mission rather than imitate competitors by default.
