# Zanistarast.com — Newroza Kawa Public Direction

Status: LOCKED — Roadmap item 2

## Purpose

Zanistarast.com is oriented toward the Newroza Kawa civilization vision while remaining understandable to an ordinary visitor who has no prior knowledge of Zanistarast terminology.

The public interface must therefore express the civilization direction primarily through understandable human concerns, stories, choices and values rather than requiring visitors to learn the theoretical vocabulary before they can use the site.

## Public-first language

Navigation, discovery and introductory content should use natural concepts such as:

- ethics and virtue
- fitrah
- justice and fairness
- mercy and compassion
- truthfulness and trust
- modesty, manners and discipline
- responsibility
- family and friendship
- courage, patience and self-restraint

These are public discovery labels, not a replacement for the canonical Zanistarast conceptual model.

Technical terms such as Hebûn, Zanabûn, Rasterast, Tek, Yek and other framework concepts may appear when the visitor deliberately goes deeper, when the source requires them, or when explaining the Zanistarast synthesis itself. They must not be sprayed across the interface merely as branding.

## Three-depth presentation

A work may expose three progressive depths:

1. **Experience** — story, film, animation, image, sound or another accessible medium.
2. **Understand** — plain-language explanation of the ethical/human question and relevant context.
3. **Deepen** — provenance, sources, scientific material where applicable, and the relevant Zanistarast/Newroza Kawa conceptual context.

A visitor may stop at any depth. Deeper layers must remain available without making the first layer academically difficult.

## Civilization-direction rule

Content selected or produced for the platform must serve the platform's ethics-and-fitrah public mission and, where Zanistarast synthesis is invoked, remain faithful to its actual definitions and epistemic boundaries.

The Newroza Kawa direction must not be implemented by forcing every story, culture, scientific result or historical source into a false Zanistarast classification. Source identity and cultural context remain intact.

## Cultural plurality

The platform may present ethical and virtuous material from many peoples, languages, periods and traditions. Their origin must be preserved rather than erased. Public presentation should make it possible to encounter a shared ethical question while still understanding where the story or work comes from.

Detailed cultural visual rules, including traditional/local dress and geographic representation, are governed by the later Cultural & Moral Visual Policy roadmap item.

## No propaganda-style UX

The product should prefer demonstration through credible stories, consequences, choices, relationships and evidence over repetitive slogans. Newroza Kawa and Zanistarast may be explained explicitly where appropriate, but the visitor experience should not depend on ideological repetition.

## Relationship to recommendations

Future recommendation and personalization systems may learn that a visitor prefers themes such as mercy, honesty or family stories. They must not redefine the civilization direction or admit content that has failed the platform's later Rasterast/content-admission gates.

## Item 2 acceptance criteria

1. Newroza Kawa is recorded as the platform's civilization direction.
2. Ordinary visitors can navigate without knowing specialist Zanistarast vocabulary.
3. Public values are discovery language, not new ontology labels.
4. Experience → Understand → Deepen provides progressive disclosure.
5. Other cultures and sources retain their identity and are not forcibly reclassified.
6. Zanistarast concepts remain available at deeper/source-appropriate levels.
7. The direction favors credible narrative and explanation over slogan-heavy presentation.
8. Recommendation systems cannot override later Rasterast/content-admission rules.
