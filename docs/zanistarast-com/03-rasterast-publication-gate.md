# Zanistarast.com — Rasterast Publication Gate

Status: LOCKED — Roadmap item 3

## Principle
No item becomes publicly publishable merely because Mira generated it, it is popular, or it exists in a source collection. Rasterast is a mandatory content-admission gate. It evaluates declared source, epistemic status, ethical purpose and Zanistarast/Newroza Kawa constraints. It does not turn interpretation into proof and does not grant scientific truth by itself.

## Required review dimensions
Every candidate must carry enough provenance for review and be checked for: source identity/type; separation of source, observation, inference, interpretation, hypothesis, fiction and adaptation; preservation of scientific evidence/status; consistency with the platform's declared Qur'an, Prophetic ethics, Risale-i Nur, Zanistarast synthesis and Newroza Kawa direction without falsely treating these sources as identical in authority/type; ethical and fitrah-oriented purpose; cultural/historical context; unresolved contradictions or missing provenance; and later legal/copyright/cultural-visual gates.

## Publication states
DRAFT — still being produced or researched.
RASTERAST_PENDING — ready for review.
NEEDS_REVISION — correctable problem found.
ESCALATED — ambiguity/conflict requires Müdebbir review.
RASTERAST_ACCEPTED — passed this gate, but is not automatically published.
REJECTED — not admissible in its current form.
PUBLISHED — all required downstream gates and human authority are satisfied.
WITHDRAWN — previously public material removed because of a later material issue.

RASTERAST_ACCEPTED must not mean scientific proof, publication, or permission to bypass later gates.

## Non-negotiable epistemic rules
- UNVERIFIED does not mean FALSE.
- Analogy does not mean identity.
- Retrieval/recommendation score does not mean authority.
- Repository presence does not mean scientific proof.
- Literary, religious, historical and scientific sources retain their source types.
- Adaptation may simplify expression but may not silently strengthen the claim.
- Counterevidence, meaningful uncertainty and known corrections must not be hidden for persuasion.

## Mira boundary
Mira may research, compare, adapt, script and prepare media candidates. Mira must not self-approve its own output. Missing provenance or unresolved meaningful conflict leads to revision/escalation, not invented certainty.

## Müdebbir boundary
Rasterast is a gate, not final sovereign authority. Judgment cases are escalated to Müdebbir. Real publication remains subject to applicable Müdebbir decision and later legal, copyright, cultural/visual and quality requirements.

## Recommendation boundary
Likes, watch time, popularity and recommendation operate only on content admitted to the appropriate public pool. They cannot upgrade rejected, pending, draft or escalated material.

## Auditability
A publishable record must be traceable to source/provenance and review state. Implementation should preserve review version/time and reasons for revision, rejection, escalation, withdrawal or correction without unnecessarily exposing private material.

## Item 3 acceptance criteria
1. Mira generation never equals publication approval.
2. Rasterast review is mandatory before public admission.
3. Source types and epistemic statuses remain distinct.
4. Rasterast acceptance is not scientific proof.
5. Uncertainty/counterevidence cannot be silently erased.
6. Ambiguous/conflicting cases can escalate to Müdebbir.
7. Likes/recommendations cannot bypass the gate.
8. Copyright/legal/cultural/quality gates remain mandatory.
9. Publication and withdrawal states are explicit and auditable.
