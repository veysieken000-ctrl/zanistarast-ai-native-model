# Zanistarast.com — Legal, Copyright and Original Mira Production Operations

Status: LOCKED — Roadmap item 31

## Principle

zanistarast.com must respect applicable law, copyright and licensing while preferring genuinely original Mira production.

Researching a work, culture, visual style, film language or storytelling method does not grant permission to copy protected expression.

Rasterast approval, Müdebbir approval and legal/rights clearance are distinct gates.

## Operational rights record

Every production candidate must have a RIGHTS_RECORD covering each material input or asset, including as applicable:

- source text;
- quotation/excerpt;
- translation;
- image/illustration;
- video footage;
- music/composition;
- sound recording;
- voice/performance;
- font or other licensed design asset;
- archive material;
- dataset/reference material whose terms affect reuse.

The record should capture source/owner when known, rights basis, license/permission evidence, scope, territory/duration where relevant, attribution requirements, restrictions, review date and asset/version linkage.

Unknown rights status is not permission.

## Permitted bases

A reusable asset may proceed only under a documented lawful basis appropriate to the intended use, such as:

- original Zanistarast/Mira production with necessary contributor rights;
- verified public domain;
- verified compatible open license;
- explicit permission/license;
- another use specifically reviewed as lawful for the concrete context.

The system must not automatically assume that old, online, religious, educational, folk or non-commercial material is copyright-free.

## Research/reference boundary

Mira may study lawful references to understand:

- cultural clothing and material culture;
- geography and architecture;
- historical context;
- cinematography;
- narrative structure;
- genre conventions;
- artistic techniques;
- comparable educational approaches.

References are evidence/inspiration inputs. The output must not closely reproduce protected characters, distinctive scenes, compositions, dialogue, music, illustrations or other protectable expression merely because Mira researched them.

## Original-production rule

For new film, animation, illustration, audio and narrative production, default to original:

research → concept → original brief → original script → original visual language → original storyboard → original production assets → edit → similarity/rights review.

Prompts such as “make it exactly like [living artist/current protected work]” are not an operational production strategy.

When a reference is useful, describe the underlying non-exclusive properties needed for the work rather than copying a protected creator's distinctive expression.

## Adaptation rule

An adaptation must record:

- source identity;
- source/adaptation relationship;
- legal basis for adaptation;
- what is retained;
- what is transformed;
- cultural/historical changes;
- attribution requirements;
- exact adapted version.

Public-domain source material does not make a newly discovered translation, recording, illustration or edition automatically public domain.

## Quotations and excerpts

Quotations/excerpts must be purpose-limited and reviewed for applicable legal basis. The platform should prefer linking/citing over reproducing unnecessary amounts of protected material.

Mira must never fabricate permission, license terms, author consent or public-domain status.

## Music and sound

Review separately:
- composition rights;
- lyrics/text rights;
- sound-recording/master rights;
- performer/voice rights;
- commissioned/original production terms.

“Royalty-free” is not equivalent to “no conditions.” License evidence and restrictions remain recorded.

Original commissioned/generated music must still receive similarity review where appropriate.

## Voice and likeness

Do not imitate or present a real person's voice/likeness as authentic without the necessary lawful basis and explicit production policy.

Synthetic voices should be traceable as production assets. Do not fabricate endorsement, participation or quotation by a real person.

## Cultural and traditional material

Traditional/folk/oral material requires provenance research even when an individual copyright owner is absent.

Review:
- whether a particular transcription/translation/recording is protected;
- community/cultural sensitivity;
- attribution and origin;
- whether sacred or restricted cultural material requires additional care.

Legal availability does not automatically mean culturally appropriate use.

## Scientific and .org material

A Zanistarast/.org source is not automatically cleared for every .com reuse merely because it is in the same repository.

Scientific transfer eligibility, third-party quotation/image rights, publication-agreement restrictions and exact source version must be checked before adaptation.

No DOI or publication/license status may be invented.

## Similarity review

Before final approval, compare original Mira output against the material references used during production and known relevant works where appropriate.

Flag material similarity in:
- wording/dialogue;
- plot/scene sequence;
- character design;
- visual composition;
- distinctive graphic elements;
- melody/audio;
- title/branding;
- thumbnails/posters.

A flag triggers review; similarity detection is not itself a legal verdict.

## Contributor and commission records

If human contributors, performers, illustrators, translators, composers or contractors participate, retain the agreements/permissions required for the intended publication and future representations.

Do not infer assignment of rights from payment alone.

## Rights-state enforcement

Operational states should include:

- RIGHTS_UNREVIEWED;
- RIGHTS_RESEARCH_ONLY;
- RIGHTS_PUBLIC_DOMAIN_VERIFIED;
- RIGHTS_OPEN_LICENSE_VERIFIED;
- RIGHTS_PERMISSION_VERIFIED;
- RIGHTS_LIMITED_USE_REVIEWED;
- RIGHTS_UNCLEAR;
- RIGHTS_RESTRICTED;
- RIGHTS_REJECTED;
- RIGHTS_REVIEW_EXPIRED.

Only states compatible with the exact intended public use may cross the publication boundary.

## Change and expiry

Rights can change operationally because:
- a license/permission expires;
- scope changes;
- a new territory/use is introduced;
- an asset is replaced;
- a claim/dispute arrives;
- previous provenance was incorrect.

Material change triggers re-review. A previously approved work does not permanently legalize every derivative.

## Claim/takedown handling

A credible rights complaint should create a traceable case:

report → identify exact asset/version → preserve evidence → assess urgency → pause/restrict when appropriate → review rights record/source → correct/replace/remove → propagate to derivatives/caches → record decision.

Immediate containment may occur without waiting for a final merits determination when risk justifies it.

A takedown is not silently erased from audit history.

## No human override of illegality

Müdebbir is the final project governance authority where required, but approval cannot turn unlawful reuse into lawful reuse.

When rights remain legally unresolved, the system must not present governance approval as legal clearance.

Specialized legal advice may be required for consequential or jurisdiction-specific questions; Mira must preserve the uncertainty rather than invent a legal conclusion.

## Evidence storage

License/permission evidence and rights records should be stored durably with controlled access. Public UI may expose necessary attribution and rights summaries without publishing private contracts or personal data.

Repository commit history alone is not sufficient evidence of third-party permission.

## Publication checklist

Before a candidate crosses the public boundary, confirm as applicable:

- all material assets identified;
- rights basis recorded;
- attribution prepared;
- license conditions satisfied;
- adaptation right checked;
- contributor/performer permissions checked;
- music/recording rights separated;
- voice/likeness reviewed;
- cultural/traditional provenance reviewed;
- similarity findings resolved;
- source/version linkage correct;
- derivatives covered;
- no fabricated license/DOI/permission;
- exact public version matches the reviewed assets.

## Item 31 acceptance criteria

1. Every material production input can have an asset-level rights record.
2. Unknown rights status is never treated as permission.
3. Research/reference access is separated from reuse rights.
4. Original Mira production is the default for new media.
5. Adaptations record source, transformation and legal basis.
6. Public-domain status is checked at the exact edition/translation/recording level where relevant.
7. Music, recording, performance and voice rights are separable.
8. Real-person voice/likeness cannot be falsely presented as authentic participation.
9. Folk/traditional material receives provenance and cultural review.
10. .org/repository presence does not automatically grant .com reuse rights.
11. Mira outputs receive similarity review before final approval.
12. Contributor/commission rights are documented rather than inferred.
13. Rights states are enforceable at the publication boundary.
14. Rights changes/claims can pause content and propagate corrections.
15. Müdebbir approval cannot legalize unlawful reuse.
16. Consequential legal uncertainty remains explicit and may require qualified legal review.
17. Rights evidence is durable and auditable.
18. Exact published assets must match the reviewed rights package.
