# Zanistarast.com — Müdebbir Final Human Authority

Status: LOCKED — Roadmap item 26

## Principle

Mira may research, synthesize, draft, generate, compare, review, flag risks and prepare publication packages. Rasterast and other automated/semi-automated gates may determine whether required conditions appear satisfied.

They do not replace the designated final human authority where this project's governance requires a Müdebbir decision.

The Müdebbir is the final authorization point for consequential publication actions reserved by the governance model. Automation must assist judgment, not silently become the judge.

## Separation of responsibilities

### Mira may
- research sources and provenance;
- prepare public adaptations;
- generate original candidate media;
- run structured checks;
- identify uncertainty/conflicts;
- compare versions;
- prepare rights/cultural/scientific/Rasterast evidence;
- recommend revision;
- assemble a decision package.

### Rasterast may
- apply its defined review methodology;
- preserve epistemic/source distinctions;
- mark unresolved items;
- accept/reject/escalate within its defined review scope.

### Müdebbir may
- inspect the exact candidate and review package;
- approve;
- reject;
- return for revision;
- require further evidence/review;
- withdraw/reconsider a prior decision when material new information arises.

No subsystem may forge or infer Müdebbir approval.

## Decision states

The governance model should support explicit states such as:

- MÜDEBBIR_NOT_REQUIRED;
- MÜDEBBIR_PENDING;
- MÜDEBBIR_NEEDS_REVISION;
- MÜDEBBIR_APPROVED;
- MÜDEBBIR_REJECTED;
- MÜDEBBIR_RECONSIDERATION_REQUIRED;
- MÜDEBBIR_WITHDRAWN.

A decision is attached to an exact WORK/REPRESENTATION/version and defined action scope.

Approval of one representation/version does not automatically approve a later changed version.

## Decision package

Where Müdebbir approval is required, Mira should present a concise but sufficient package containing, as applicable:

- work identity and exact version;
- public representation preview;
- purpose/audience;
- source/provenance graph;
- adaptation relationship;
- scientific eligibility/status;
- Rasterast report and unresolved items;
- Qur'anic-ruling/Prophetic-ethics compatibility review state under this project's framework;
- cultural/historical review;
- Cultural & Moral Visual Policy review;
- copyright/rights basis;
- translation status;
- accessibility status;
- AI/Mira production disclosure;
- corrections/previous decisions;
- material risks/uncertainties;
- proposed publication target/action.

The package must not hide negative evidence merely to obtain approval.

## Human-readable decision

Müdebbir must be able to understand what is being approved without reading raw machine logs. Technical evidence may remain available as deeper detail.

The interface should make material uncertainty, blocking issues and changed content conspicuous.

## Action boundary

Actions reserved for Müdebbir must fail closed without a valid approval. Examples may include:

- final public release of governed works;
- real external submission/publication where reserved;
- reinstatement after a consequential withdrawal;
- approval of escalated religious/source/cultural cases;
- other actions explicitly marked MÜDEBBIR_REQUIRED.

A background job, recommendation service, administrator shortcut or provider callback cannot bypass this boundary.

## No blanket approval

Müdebbir approval must not be interpreted as:
- approval of all future versions;
- approval of all works by the same author/source;
- scientific proof;
- copyright ownership;
- independent religious authority;
- permission to skip later corrections/re-review.

It is an explicit governance authorization for the recorded scope/version.

## Religious uncertainty

Within this project's declared framework, no published content may conflict with applicable Qur'anic rulings and Prophetic ethics.

Mira must not fabricate a religious ruling, and Müdebbir review must be shown the unresolved source/interpretive issue when one exists rather than receiving an artificially certain summary.

Where appropriate expertise/source verification is still required, the correct state is further review/evidence, not forced approval.

## Revisions after decision

A post-approval change must be classified:

- non-material technical change;
- minor non-meaning correction;
- material content/meaning change.

Material changes invalidate the prior approval for the changed version and return it through affected review gates and Müdebbir when required.

## Emergency containment

Authorized systems may pause or withdraw content immediately when necessary to contain a credible material issue. Emergency removal does not require waiting for a publication approval decision.

Re-publication after a consequential issue requires the applicable corrected-version reviews and Müdebbir decision.

## Audit

Every Müdebbir decision should retain:

- decision ID;
- actor identity;
- exact work/representation/version;
- action scope;
- decision state;
- timestamp;
- concise rationale/notes;
- evidence/review package reference;
- superseded/reconsidered decision relationship.

Audit history must not be silently rewritten.

## Authentication and authorization

The technical implementation must require strong authentication/authorization for Müdebbir actions. Possessing ordinary editor/reviewer access must not imply Müdebbir authority.

Secrets/tokens must not be exposed in public logs, UI payloads or repository content.

## Availability and delegation

If the designated Müdebbir is unavailable, the system remains pending unless an explicit future governance rule defines authorized delegation. Mira must not invent a substitute decision-maker.

Any future delegation must be explicit, scoped, authenticated and auditable.

## Item 26 acceptance criteria

1. Mira assists but cannot grant Müdebbir approval.
2. Rasterast and Müdebbir remain distinct governance roles.
3. Decisions bind to exact versions and action scope.
4. Required decisions fail closed when absent.
5. Decision packages expose positive and negative evidence.
6. Approval is not scientific proof, copyright ownership or blanket religious authority.
7. Material post-approval changes require affected re-review/re-approval.
8. Emergency containment may remove content immediately; reinstatement remains governed.
9. Religious/source uncertainty is preserved rather than fabricated away.
10. Müdebbir actions require strong authorization.
11. Decisions are auditable and cannot be silently rewritten.
12. No automated service can forge/infer approval.
13. No substitute Müdebbir is invented when the designated authority is unavailable.
