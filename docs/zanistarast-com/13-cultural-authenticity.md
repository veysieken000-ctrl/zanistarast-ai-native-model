# Zanistarast.com — Cultural Authenticity and Context Integrity

Status: LOCKED — Roadmap item 13

## Principle

When a work belongs to, depicts or adapts a particular people, culture, place or historical period, zanistarast.com must preserve that identity rather than replacing it with a generic contemporary aesthetic.

Cultural authenticity is a research and review obligation. It is not permission to stereotype, romanticize, demean, erase or falsely homogenize a people.

## Context record

A culturally situated work/representation should be able to record, where known and relevant:

- people/community/culture;
- geography and locality;
- historical period/date range;
- language/dialect;
- source tradition and provenance;
- architecture and settlement context;
- clothing/textiles/adornment;
- material culture and everyday objects;
- food, work and social setting when relevant;
- landscape/climate/season;
- customs and social practices;
- music/sound tradition where legally and historically appropriate;
- uncertainty, regional variation and conflicting evidence.

Unknown details remain unknown; Mira must not fabricate specificity merely to make a scene look complete.

## Research before generation

For culturally specific media, the production pipeline should gather appropriate references before script visualization or asset generation. Research should prefer traceable, context-appropriate material and distinguish historical evidence from later reconstruction, tourism imagery or modern stylization.

Visual reference is for understanding reality and producing an original work. It is not permission to copy a protected photograph, costume design, film frame, illustration, recording or other protected expression.

## Clothing and appearance

Clothing must be selected according to the represented people, locality, period, social context and the platform's later Cultural & Moral Visual Policy.

Traditional/local clothing should not be treated as a single costume shared by an entire people across all regions and centuries. Where evidence supports multiple forms, the production may choose an appropriate form and retain the contextual rationale.

Within this project's governing framework, the final depiction must also satisfy the applicable Qur'anic-ruling, Prophetic-ethics and edep/haya requirements. Cultural authenticity cannot be invoked to bypass the publication boundary; where historical truth and presentation constraints create a difficult case, it must be reviewed rather than silently rewritten.

## Architecture, geography and environment

A story should not be visually relocated merely because generic assets are easier to generate. Buildings, settlement patterns, terrain, vegetation, weather and material surroundings should match the known context closely enough to avoid misleading the audience.

Stylization is permitted when clearly artistic, but it must not falsely claim documentary/historical accuracy.

## Language, names and dialogue

Names, forms of address, pronunciation, idiom and dialogue should be researched where they materially affect authenticity. Translation may make dialogue understandable to the audience while preserving names and culturally meaningful concepts when appropriate.

Mira must not invent a quotation, proverb, ritual, religious practice or folk custom and attribute it to a real people without support.

## Historical persons and events

For real historical persons/events, documented historical evidence has priority over the platform's default aesthetic preferences. Fictional reconstruction must be distinguishable from established fact where the distinction matters.

Composite characters or dramatized scenes may be used only when their status is not misleading and when the work's source/adaptation record permits it.

## Other peoples and traditions

No people should be relabeled as another culture for visual convenience or assimilated into Zanistarast terminology. External traditions can be presented under their own names, provenance and context.

The platform may draw ethical comparisons or public explanations after admission/review, but comparison does not establish identity or ownership.

## Internal diversity

Cultural metadata should allow regional, linguistic, temporal, religious and social variation where relevant. One example must not silently become a universal statement about an entire people.

When sources conflict, the work should preserve the uncertainty or choose a defensible interpretation with an auditable rationale.

## Review gate

Before publication of culturally situated media, applicable review should check:

1. source/provenance integrity;
2. people/place/period identification;
3. clothing and material culture;
4. architecture/geography/environment;
5. language/names/customs where relevant;
6. absence of unsupported stereotypes or invented attribution;
7. originality/copyright compliance;
8. consistency with the platform's governing moral publication constraints;
9. disclosure of meaningful dramatization or uncertainty.

Material errors return the representation for revision. Ambiguous consequential cases may be escalated through Rasterast and Müdebbir review.

## Item 13 acceptance criteria

1. Cultural identity is preserved for culturally situated works.
2. People, geography, period and language can be recorded separately.
3. Clothing, architecture and environment are researched contextually.
4. Regional/temporal diversity is not flattened into one costume or stereotype.
5. Unknown cultural details are not fabricated.
6. Historical evidence is distinguished from reconstruction/stylization.
7. Mira cannot invent and attribute customs, quotations or traditions to a people.
8. Other peoples retain their own names and provenance.
9. Research references do not grant permission to copy protected expression.
10. Historical truth takes priority over default aesthetics for real events/persons.
11. Cultural authenticity remains subject to the project's Qur'anic-ruling, Prophetic-ethics and edep/haya publication boundary.
12. Cultural review is auditable and can return media for revision.
